Project Context

Conducted a comprehensive enterprise risk assessment for Industrias Vaine (fictitious defense contractor), specifically for the Proyecto Alfredo de Gotan — an AI-powered defense prototype initiative.

The assessment identified information assets, threat vectors, and vulnerabilities across the project lifecycle, applying a structured risk management approach aligned with ISO 31000 and mapped to ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls.

ISO 31000 ISO 27001:2022 eMarisma Risk Treatment Plans

Methodology

Risk assessment was performed using the eMarisma risk analysis engine, a structured methodology that systematically identifies, evaluates, and prioritizes information security risks:

1. Asset Identification & Valuation

Cataloged all information assets within the project scope (AI models, defense data, prototype schematics, development infrastructure) and assigned valuation based on confidentiality, integrity, and availability criteria.

2. Threat & Vulnerability Analysis

Identified threat sources (external adversaries, insider threats, natural disasters, technical failures) and mapped vulnerabilities per asset using eMarisma’s threat taxonomy.

3. Risk Evaluation (Probability × Impact)

Calculated risk levels using the eMarisma risk matrix, producing qualitative risk ratings for each asset-threat-vulnerability combination.

4. Control Mapping (ISO 27001:2022)

Mapped each identified risk to relevant ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls, identifying gaps where existing controls were insufficient.

5. Risk Treatment Plans (PTR)

Developed structured Risk Treatment Plans (Planes de Tratamiento de Riesgos) for each unacceptable risk, specifying mitigation actions, responsible parties, timelines, and residual risk acceptance criteria.

Risk Treatment Plans (PTR)

For each unacceptable risk, a structured PTR was developed specifying:

PTR Structure Elements

  • Risk reference and current risk level
  • Treatment option selected: Mitigate, Transfer, Accept, or Avoid
  • Specific ISO 27001:2022 controls selected for implementation
  • Detailed mitigation actions and control implementation steps
  • Expected residual risk after treatment
  • Responsible party and implementation timeline
  • Verification and monitoring procedures

Key Controls Applied

  • A.6.1.2 — Information security in project management
  • A.8.1.1 — Inventory of information and associated processing facilities
  • A.8.2.1 — Classification of information
  • A.8.3.1 — Physical media handling (physical controls for prototypes)
  • A.5.14 — Information transfer (secure communications)
  • A.5.23 — Information security in cloud services
  • A.8.9 — Configuration management
  • A.8.28 — Secure coding (secure AI development practices)

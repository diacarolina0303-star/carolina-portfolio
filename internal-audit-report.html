2. Audit Objectives & Scope

2.1. Objectives

  • Verify compliance with [applicable framework/standard] controls
  • Assess the effectiveness of implemented security controls
  • Identify gaps, weaknesses, and areas for improvement
  • Evaluate the maturity of security governance processes
  • Provide actionable remediation recommendations

2.2. Scope

[Define what was in scope and out of scope. Example:]

  • In Scope: Access control processes (A.5.15–A.8.2), vulnerability management (A.8.8), data classification (A.8.10), incident management (A.5.24–A.5.28)
  • Out of Scope: Physical security controls (audited separately), third-party cloud infrastructure (covered in TPRM audit)

2.3. Methodology

  • Document review (policies, procedures, evidence artifacts)
  • Interviews with process owners and key personnel
  • Technical testing (access reviews, vulnerability scan validation, configuration checks)
  • Sampling of controls across defined scope
